# 星光玩具岛 · POP PLAYLAND

An explorable 3D toy island populated by eight Twinkle Twinkle-inspired star characters and eight Labubu-inspired characters. All models and scenery are procedural fan artwork.

The starlight park update opens a larger island with 3.31 times the original area, eleven districts, lakeside paths, gardens, an observatory and a moon library. Night is the default: warm lamps, illuminated windows, forty-two suspended stars, a crescent moon and falling star rain light the park. Eight star friends gather at the fountain for a choreographed constellation show; use **星光秀** to watch or end the performance.

Use **夜游指南** to walk to attractions. Ride the carousel, cloud swing, Ferris wheel or moon boat, with your character moving in its actual seat. Enter the star bedroom, forest house and cloud bakery to explore their furnished interiors. Rest on a bed, sit on a sofa or garden bench, look through a telescope, or bake a cookie. Use **下车**, **起身** or **回到小岛** to leave an activity. The light button switches between day and night.

The atelier update adds sixteen distinct outfit silhouettes, smooth sculpted faces, glossy irises, embroidered and knitted fabrics, denim seams, fine plush pile, articulated ears, moving capes and layered wings. Use **细看朋友** for a portrait camera; the friend book and wardrobe show real 3D model portraits.

Each character has an individual personality and idle repertoire: yawning, napping, saluting, adjusting glasses, examining a flower, nibbling a donut, bowing, giggling, stretching and more. Interaction expressions ease between states, with asymmetric winks, curious eye movement, happy cheek blush and surprise. Dance and photo poses vary by character.

Click the ground to walk. Click a character to approach and greet them, then use the action buttons to wave, pet, hug, dance, walk together, or pose for a souvenir photograph. Choose any of sixteen character appearances, meet all sixteen friends, collect twenty-four sparks, make a wish, and pick up a star balloon. Buildings, furniture and water constrain navigation; bridges connect the lakeside paths. Each interior has its own walkable floor plan.

- Mouse: click to move/interact, drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.
- Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys to walk, Space to hop, E to interact or leave a seat, C to photograph.
- Touch: tap to move/interact, drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, or use the movement joystick.
- Friendships, collected sparks, souvenirs, appearance, sound and light preferences stay in this browser's local storage.
- Photo mode captures the actual rendered scene and offers a PNG download.

The app bundles Three.js and its assets and makes no third-party requests. No account or payment is required. Friendly interaction sounds are synthesized locally; no official voice lines are included.
