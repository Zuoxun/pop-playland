# Character design references

The island's characters are original procedural fan interpretations. Product photographs were consulted for silhouette, clothing construction and recognizable facial features. They are not bundled with the app, and the game does not distribute official meshes or claim catalogue accuracy.

## 星星人 / Twinkle Twinkle

The official [Sweet Dreams Forecast collection](https://www.popmart.com/us/products/3450) provided references for Keep Shining, Mess in Wind, Rain on Me, Flash Warning, Afterglow Marshmallow and Hug from Snowman: striped socks, a mint rain poncho, a lightning motif, rainbow plush and a rounded snowman outfit. The island adds a pilot and a baker interpretation inspired by the Wonderful Journey and Create Your Taste themes.

Official collection links: [Twinkle Twinkle](https://www.popmart.com/us/products/3450), [POP MART plush collection](https://www.popmart.com/us/collection/20).

The living island refinement studies the official Keep Shining, Rain on Me and Hug from Snowman product images for big glossy white eyes, deep pupils with bright highlights, rosy circular cheeks, a tiny blue teardrop nose, a delicate mouth, a fluffy hood opening, striped long socks, a pointed mint rain hood and embroidered clothing. It retains eight distinct outfit interpretations and uses actual articulated geometry for dancing and park activities.

## LABUBU / THE MONSTERS

The official [Labubu collection](https://www.popmart.com/us/collection/143/labubu-plush) lists Exciting Macaron, FLIP WITH ME, FALL IN WILD, Time to Chill, ROCK THE UNIVERSE, Let’s Checkmate, ANGEL IN CLOUDS and other themes. The island uses these references for fluffy hoods, striped shirts, glasses, denim overalls, outdoor hats, crowns, capes and wings. The space helmet and mushroom explorer are imaginative extensions for the island.

Clothing photo references consulted:

- [Outdoor / striped shirt / overalls comparison](https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/YhMAAeSwo4NooATj/s-l1200.png)
- [Sweet Dreams weather collection](https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/RfMAAeSwOKhopT41/s-l1200.png)
- [POP MART official weather product gallery](https://www.popmart.com/us/products/3450)

The face refinement also uses the outdoor / striped shirt / overalls comparison above to study the broad rounded vinyl face, softly sculpted brows, large dark oval pupils, tiny triangular nose and the nine short teeth beneath a thin closed smile. The procedural eyes, smile and teeth conform to the curved face rather than projecting a deep open mouth. The reference photographs remain local review material and are not distributed in the game.

Character identities and original product designs belong to their respective creators and rights holders. This project is unofficial and is not affiliated with POP MART. References reviewed 2026-11-07.
